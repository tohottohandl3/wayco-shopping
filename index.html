About Wayco

A new kind of shopping experience.

Wayco Shopping is a Roblox-based shopping brand focused on creating realistic, enjoyable and accessible places for players to visit.

Founded with the goal of creating something different, Wayco Shopping is developing a modern shopping environment where players can explore stores, interact with staff and spend time with friends.

Following our move away from our previous single-store project, Wayco represents a new direction for the company. Rather than focusing on one brand, we're building a wider shopping destination with room to grow.

Our aim is simple: create a place that feels familiar, professional and enjoyable every time you visit.
